Your privacy

What Hints keeps, who can see it, and how to delete it. Updated November 2026.

The short version

  • Hints is free, has no ads, and never sells or shares your information.
  • Only people in your circles see your list and profile. Nothing is public unless you make a share link.
  • The person a gift is for never sees who's getting it. Hints doesn't just hide it on screen: it never sends it to them.
  • Your face, profile and personal details are never sent to any AI.
  • You can delete everything yourself, any time, in Settings.

What Hints keeps

  • What you type in: your name, wishes, profile, ideas, budgets and chat messages.
  • Photos you add, shrunk down and kept in Hints' own database. Product photos come from the store's link and aren't copied.
  • Your 4-digit PIN, kept only as a scrambled code (a hash). Nobody can read it back, not even the person who runs Hints.
  • A sign-in token on each phone or computer you use, kept in that browser so you stay signed in.
  • If you turn on notifications, the address your browser gives Hints for sending them.
  • If the app hits an error, a short report (what went wrong, which screen, which browser) goes to the person who runs Hints so they can fix it.

Hints has no analytics, no tracking pixels and no ad cookies.

Where it lives

  • Hints runs on Cloudflare. Their terms say they don't use what's stored there to train AI.
  • Everything is backed up so a mistake can be undone. Weekly backups are encrypted.
  • Like the owner of any app, the person who runs Hints can get to the database to fix things.

AI, only to find products

Some helpers use Google's Gemini to work out what a product is from its link, guess a price, tidy a name, or match a photo you take of a product. Only things about the gift are sent (the link, the product's name, brand, size or color, or your product photo). Never who it's for, and never anything about you.

Hints pays for Gemini, and under Google's paid terms what's sent isn't used to train their AI. Google keeps it only briefly to catch abuse.

Your profile photo, name, birthday, sizes, loves, allergies and pet or household photos are never sent to any AI.

Signing in

Everyone has a 4-digit PIN. A circle's invite link plus your name isn't enough to sign in as you: it also takes your PIN, or your own personal sign-in link. Lost a phone? Settings > Sign out everywhere else. Forgot your PIN? Someone in your household or your circle's admin can clear it so you can pick a new one, and you'll see who did.

Deleting your information

  • Everything: Settings > Delete my Hints. Your profile, list, ideas, budgets, purchases, chat messages, photos and sign-ins are deleted for good. Circles you run get a new admin.
  • One thing: remove a wish, idea, photo or message any time. Chat messages are deleted after 6 months anyway.
  • Leaving a circle: Settings > Circle > Leave. That circle stops seeing your list.

Questions

Signed in? Use Settings > Questions or problems. It goes straight to the person who runs Hints.
